📜 Rules of play

  • Each pair records one team score per hole.
  • Only one nominated player contributes to the team score at each pub.
  • Lowest overall score wins.

  • Any misconduct, infringement or punishment is at the discretion of the Officiating Team. Their decision is final.

  • Bogey: one over par
